Planning Desk

A quieter way to plan the week together

Replace scattered updates with one shared note that gives the team a clear Monday starting point.

ME

Mara Ellison

March 12, 2025 · Updated March 14 · 7 min read

Start with what changed

Carry forward only unfinished work, recent decisions, and deadlines that moved. The team starts with context instead of a blank page.

Three lines are usually enough: what shifted, why it matters, and who needs to respond.

Give every priority an owner

Name one owner and one next action for each priority. This makes the next handoff visible without assigning all the work to one person.

End with a 15-minute Thursday review so the team can adjust while there is still time to act.
