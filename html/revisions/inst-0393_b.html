Employer administration

Benefits Administration Terms

Administrator For authorized employer representatives
Employer information access

Your organization may access eligibility records, enrollment status, billing data, and de-identified program reports. Administrators cannot access a member’s journal, messages, coaching conversations, or individual health measurements.

Administrator responsibilities

Access and data

Sections 1–2
  1. 01
    Authorized use.

    Access is limited to benefits administration, eligibility support, and approved program reporting.

  2. 02
    Confidentiality.

    Protect account credentials and do not attempt to identify members from aggregated reports.

Program operations

Sections 3–4
  1. 03
    Accurate eligibility.

    Keep roster and coverage information current and correct errors promptly.

  2. 04
    Voluntary participation.

    Do not require health disclosures or use Viora activity for employment decisions.
