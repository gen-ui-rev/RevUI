Remote Critiques for Junior Designers

A lightweight format that makes feedback useful without turning the call into a performance.

Noah Kim

Design operations · May 2, 2025 · 4 min read

Junior designers need room to explain intent before senior voices frame the solution. A short sequence keeps the critique focused and fair.

A compact critique rhythm

Before

  • Send the frame.
  • Name the decision.

During

  • Start with intent.
  • Gather chat notes.

After

  • Share the recap.
  • Offer follow-up.
