Workout plan

Lower-body stability

Steady Strength

Move through each phase from top to bottom. Rest only where noted.

Time 24 min
Level Beginner
Equipment Chair, mini band and mat

Your sequence

6 movements
Phase 1 Prepare 4 min

Chair-supported march

Stand tall and alternate knees

60 sec

Hip hinge reach

Push hips back with a long spine

10 reps
Phase 2 Build 16 min

Banded squat

Press knees gently into the band

12 reps

Supported split squat

Use the chair for balance

8 / side

Glute bridge

Pause for one count at the top

12 reps
Phase 3 Reset 4 min

Figure-four stretch

Breathe slowly and switch sides

45 sec

Complete the Build phase twice, resting 45 seconds between rounds.
