Quiet Current

How Songs Learn to End

Episode 42 · 31:18

The ending feels longer than it is because every instrument leaves at a different moment. The piano stays just long enough to make the room around it audible.

At 24:36, Rowan Hale describes silence as part of the arrangement rather than an empty space. That is the idea I want to remember.

Listen again with headphones and mark the point where the final note becomes room tone.
