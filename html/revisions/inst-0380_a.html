Please read this form before joining. It explains the study, what participation involves, and whom to contact with questions.

1. Purpose of the study

Larkline Learning, Inc. is studying whether personalized review schedules improve vocabulary retention in adult language learners.

2. What participation involves

You will complete ordinary app lessons, brief weekly memory checks, and two online surveys. Total added time is about 20 minutes per week.

3. Possible risks and benefits

Risks are limited to mild fatigue or frustration. You may not benefit directly, but your participation may improve future learning tools.

4. Privacy and study records

Your responses will be assigned a study code. Reports will use grouped results and will not include your name or contact details.

5. Voluntary participation

Joining is your choice. You may stop at any time without losing access to your Larkline account, lessons, or subscription benefits.

6. Questions and contacts

Contact the research team at research@larkline.example or the independent ethics office at ethics@larkline.example.
