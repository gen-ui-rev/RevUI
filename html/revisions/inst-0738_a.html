Renal Diet Guide

Ginger Lentil & Cauliflower Bowl

A measured lentil portion with low-sodium seasoning and crisp vegetables.

30 minutes Easy 2 portions
310 mg Sodium
510 mg Potassium
260 mg Phosphorus
18 g Protein

Kidney-smart ingredients

Per 2 portions
  • Cooked lentils, rinsed 1 cup
  • Cauliflower rice 2 cups
  • Shredded green cabbage 1 cup
  • Red bell pepper ½ cup
  • Ginger-lemon dressing 3 tbsp

Preparation

3 steps

  1. Rinse and warm

    Rinse lentils well, then warm them with ginger and two tablespoons of water.

  2. Cook the vegetables

    Sauté cauliflower, cabbage, and pepper until tender-crisp; do not add salt.

  3. Portion the bowls

    Divide evenly and finish each bowl with lemon dressing and black pepper.

Care note Potassium and protein needs vary. Follow the portions set by your renal dietitian.
