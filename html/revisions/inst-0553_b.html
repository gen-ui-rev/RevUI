Lisbon lemon rice

From the Alfama Market Walk · Lisbon, Portugal

35 minutes • Easy

Scale recipe Amounts update by serving

Ingredients 6 items

  • ½ cup 1 cup 1½ cups Long-grain rice
  • 1 tbsp 2 tbsp 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • ½ small 1 small 1½ small Yellow onion, diced
  • 1 clove 2 cloves 3 cloves Garlic, minced
  • ¾ cup 1½ cups 2¼ cups Vegetable broth
  • ½ 1 1½ Lemon, zest and juice

Cooking steps

1

Soften the aromatics

Cook onion in warm oil for 5 minutes; add garlic.

2

Toast and simmer

Add rice and broth; cover and simmer for 18 minutes.

3

Rest and finish

Rest for 5 minutes, then fluff and add lemon.
