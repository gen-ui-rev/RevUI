Your workplace wellness benefit

Employee Wellness Terms

For employees enrolled through their employer

Effective June 1, 2025 · Version 3.2

What your employer can access

Your employer may see enrollment status, benefit eligibility, and aggregated participation reports. It cannot see your journal entries, messages, or individual health readings unless you authorize sharing or disclosure is legally required.

Your participation

Privacy and workplace separation

Your personal wellness activity is handled under the Privacy Policy and is not used for employment decisions by Viora.

Wellness support, not medical care

Plans and insights support general wellness and do not replace diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.

Account responsibility

Use accurate information, protect your sign-in details, and notify support if you suspect unauthorized access.

Eligibility and safe use

Access depends on your employer’s benefit plan. Stop activity if you feel unwell and seek qualified care when needed.

You can stop participating without affecting your employment.
