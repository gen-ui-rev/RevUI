Ginger Sesame Noodles

25 minutes · Serves 2 · Easy

Mara Ellison

Shared in Weeknight Table

Ingredients

Grouped by risk · 6 items
  • Flagged for you 3
  • Dried wheat noodles Wheat 7 oz
  • Low-sodium soy sauce Soy 2 tbsp
  • Toasted sesame oil Sesame 1 tbsp
  • Cleared for you 3
  • Fresh ginger, grated 2 tsp
  • Rice vinegar 1 tbsp
  • Scallions, thinly sliced 2

Cooking steps

4 steps
  1. 1

    Boil the noodles until tender, reserving ¼ cup of the cooking water.

  2. 2

    Whisk sesame oil, soy sauce, ginger, and rice vinegar in a serving bowl.

  3. 3

    Add the hot noodles with 2 tablespoons cooking water and toss to coat.

  4. 4

    Divide into bowls and finish with scallions. Serve warm.
