Method

4 steps · 30 minutes
1

Heat the broiler. Whisk the miso, mirin, and rice vinegar in a small bowl until smooth.

2

Simmer the barley in the stock, covered, for 20 minutes. Fold in the spinach off the heat.

3

Pat the salmon dry, brush half the glaze over the tops, and broil 8 minutes on the upper rack.

4

Spoon on the remaining glaze and rest 3 minutes before plating over the barley.
