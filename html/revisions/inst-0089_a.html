Endurance Recovery

The recovery lunch that
rebuilds long-run legs

How marathoners and cyclists turn the meal after training into real adaptation.

PR

Priya Raman, RD, CSSD

June 4, 2025 · 6 min read

After two hours of steady effort, muscle glycogen sits low and protein turnover runs high. Lunch is the widest window you get: carbohydrate to reload the tank, protein to repair fibers, and fluid to replace what you lost in sweat.

Recovery ratio

Aim for 1.0–1.2 g of carbohydrate per kg of body weight with 30 g of protein, inside 60 minutes.

Build the plate, then drink to it

Start with rice, potatoes, or pasta, then add salmon, chicken, or lentils and a salty side to hold on to fluid. Finish with 500–700 ml of water or milk, and weigh in later to confirm you replaced the sweat loss.
