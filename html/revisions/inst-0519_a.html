How sleep debt builds up over a single week

NR
Dr. Naomi Reyes
Reviewed February 12, 2026 · 6 min read

The short version

Losing 45 minutes a night sounds minor, but by Friday it adds up to a full night of missed sleep — and one long weekend morning does not clear it.

Sleep debt is the running difference between the sleep your body needs and the sleep it actually gets. For most adults that need sits between seven and nine hours, and it stays roughly constant from night to night.

The debt accumulates quietly. Reaction time and mood slip first, usually before people notice feeling tired, which is why self-reported alertness is a poor guide to how rested you are.
