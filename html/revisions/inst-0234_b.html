Your health data, and what Daybreak does with it

Effective March 4, 2025 · About 9 minutes to read

The short version

Three things worth knowing before the fine print.

What we collect

Only what you log — meals, workouts and sleep — plus readings from devices you connect.

What we never do

We do not sell your record, and no advertiser or data broker ever receives it.

What you control

Export or permanently erase your entire record from Settings, at any time.

What your record contains

Meal photos
