Policy summary

Sprigwell uses personal information to provide lessons, save progress, and secure learner accounts. We do not sell personal information.

1

Information we collect

Gathered when you sign up and study

Account and learning data

Email address, age range, course progress, quiz results, and study preferences.

Device and usage data

Device type, app version, lesson activity, and diagnostic records used for reliability.

2

How we use that information

Only for the purposes listed here

Teaching and saved progress

Delivering lessons, restoring your place in a course, and recommending practice.

Security and reliability

Verifying sign-ins, preventing abuse, and fixing crashes reported by the app.

How long we keep it

We retain information while it is needed for these purposes, then remove or de-identify it in line with legal and security requirements.

3

Your privacy choices

Access, download, or delete

Request a copy of your information or close your account from Profile settings.
