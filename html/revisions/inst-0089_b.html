The first two hours

  1. 0–30 min

    Sip 500 ml with electrolytes and take 60 g of fast carbs while you cool down.

  2. 30–60 min

    Sit down to the full plate: grains, lean protein, cooked vegetables.

  3. 2 hours

    Add yogurt and fruit if a second session lands the same day.

Sample recovery plate

Teriyaki salmon over jasmine rice with edamame, cucumber salad, and tart cherry juice.
