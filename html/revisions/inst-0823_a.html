Book Talks That Welcome Every Reader

A classroom routine that helps reflective students enter the conversation with confidence.

Dana Brooks

Literacy coach · April 9, 2025 · 3 min read

Students contribute more thoughtfully when they know the question, have time to gather evidence, and can respond in more than one way.

Plan the conversation in three moments

Before

  • Post one open question.
  • Ask students to mark a passage.

During

  • Begin with silent note-making.
  • Invite each table before repeats.

After

  • Collect one written reflection.
  • Name an idea to revisit.
