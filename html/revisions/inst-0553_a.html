Lisbon lemon rice

From the Alfama Market Walk · Lisbon, Portugal

• 35 minutes • Easy

Ingredients 6 items

  • 1 cup Long-grain rice
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 small Yellow onion, diced
  • 2 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1½ cups Vegetable broth
  • 1 Lemon, zest and juice

Cooking steps

1

Soften the aromatics

Cook onion in warm oil for 5 minutes; add garlic.

2

Toast and simmer

Toast rice 1 minute. Add broth; cover and simmer 18 minutes.

3

Rest and finish

Rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff and add lemon.
