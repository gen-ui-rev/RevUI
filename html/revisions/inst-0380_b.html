Vocabulary Retention and Adaptive Review Study

Version 3.1

6 Sections

You are invited to take part in a research study operated by Larkline Learning, Inc. Review the information below before deciding whether to participate.

Principal investigator Dr. Maya Ellison
Study duration 12 weeks
Additional time About 20 minutes weekly
01

Why this study is being done

We are testing whether personalized review schedules help adult language learners remember vocabulary for longer periods.

02

What you will be asked to do

You will complete normal app lessons, short weekly memory checks, and surveys at the beginning and end of the study.

03

Risks and discomforts

You may experience mild fatigue or frustration during memory checks. You may pause a task or stop participating at any time.

04

Benefits and compensation

You may not benefit directly. Participants who complete the study receive three months of Larkline Plus at no charge.

05

Privacy and confidentiality

Your study data will use a coded identifier. Published findings will present grouped results and will not identify you.

06

Your participation is voluntary

You may decline or withdraw without penalty. Your decision will not affect your account, subscription, or access to lessons.

Questions? Contact research@larkline.example. For concerns about participant rights, contact the independent ethics office at ethics@larkline.example.
