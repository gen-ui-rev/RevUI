Planning Desk

A quieter way to plan the week together

Replace scattered updates with one shared note that gives the team a clear Monday starting point.

ME

Mara Ellison

Published Mar 12, 2025 Updated Mar 14 7 min read

Start with what changed

Carry forward unfinished work, recent decisions, and deadlines that moved. This gives the team context without rebuilding the plan.

Keep it to three lines: what shifted, why it matters, and who needs to respond.

Make the next handoff visible

Name one owner and one next action for each priority, then set a short Thursday review so adjustments happen in time.
