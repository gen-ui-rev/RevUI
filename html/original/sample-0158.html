Ginger Sesame Noodles

25 minutes · Serves 2 · Easy

Mara Ellison

Shared in Weeknight Table

Ingredients

6 items
  • Dried wheat noodles 7 oz
  • Toasted sesame oil 1 tbsp
  • Low-sodium soy sauce 2 tbsp
  • Fresh ginger, grated 2 tsp
  • Rice vinegar 1 tbsp
  • Scallions, thinly sliced 2

Cooking steps

4 steps
  1. 1

    Cook the noodles in salted water until tender. Reserve ¼ cup cooking water, then drain.

  2. 2

    Whisk sesame oil, soy sauce, ginger, and rice vinegar in a large serving bowl.

  3. 3

    Add hot noodles and 2 tablespoons cooking water. Toss until the sauce coats every strand.

  4. 4

    Divide between two bowls and finish with scallions. Serve warm or at room temperature.
