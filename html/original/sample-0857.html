Allow notifications to get dose reminders

Marrow Health uses notifications to alert you when a dose is due and to confirm what you logged. Nothing else is sent.

Dose reminders 8:00 AM, 9:30 PM
Missed dose follow-up 30 min after
Weekly adherence recap Sundays

The system will ask once. You can change reminder times or turn them off in Settings at any time.
