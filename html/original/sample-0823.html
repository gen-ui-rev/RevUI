How Small Groups Make Room for Quieter Voices

A practical way to keep fast conversations from leaving people behind.

Mara Ellison

Community editor · March 18, 2025 · 4 min read

In a lively group, speed can look like confidence. People who need a moment to think may have ideas ready only after the topic has moved on.

Change the rhythm, not the people

Before opening discussion, give everyone a minute to write. Then invite one thought from each person before anyone speaks twice. The pause lowers the cost of entering the conversation.

A good pause is part of the discussion, not an interruption to it.

A simple three-part practice

  1. 1Share the question before the meeting starts.
  2. 2Begin with one quiet minute for notes.
  3. 3Close by asking whose view is still missing.
