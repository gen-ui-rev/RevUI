Three Slow Days on the Cliff Road

MR
Marisol Reyes
March 14, 2025 · 9 min read

The bus from Sorrento drops you at a bend with no name, where the road narrows to one lane and the lemon terraces fall away toward the water. From here, the only way down is on foot.

Book the room with the shutters, not the view. Here they are the same thing.

Casa Lentini has six rooms and one staircase; breakfast is figs, bread, and coffee at a shared table above the harbor.
