Price Watch

Know the moment a price drops

Price Watch keeps following a listing after you close it. When the seller lowers the price, you get one notification with the new total.

How it works
1

Tap the tag icon on any listing

It sits next to the price, above the seller name.

2

Name the price you would pay

Set $34.00 on a $41.50 jacket and we watch for it.

3

Get one alert, shipping included

Nothing further until the price moves again.

Turn on Price Watch

Watching 0 of 20 listings. Turn off anytime in Settings.
