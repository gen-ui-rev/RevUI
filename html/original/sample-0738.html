Ginger Lentil Power Bowl

Warm lentils, roasted vegetables, greens, and tahini.

35 minutes Easy 2 servings
520 Calories
24 g Protein
17 g Fiber
18 g Fat

Ingredients

For 2 servings
  • Cooked green lentils 1½ cups
  • Sweet potato, cubed 1 medium
  • Baby spinach 2 cups
  • Tahini 2 tbsp
  • Fresh ginger, grated 2 tsp

Directions

4 steps

  1. Roast the sweet potato

    Bake at 425°F with olive oil and salt for 20 minutes.

  2. Warm the lentils

    Heat with ginger and 2 tablespoons of water for 4 minutes.

  3. Mix the dressing

    Whisk tahini with lemon juice and warm water until smooth.

  4. Build the bowls

    Divide spinach, lentils, and potato; finish with dressing.
